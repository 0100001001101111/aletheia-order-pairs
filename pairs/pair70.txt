Bottoms are 0..6. A has one separate top for every singleton, pair and triple of bottoms. Subsets are sorted first by size and then lexicographically; top 7+k is above exactly subset k. B keeps the singletons and pairs in the same order. Its next 35 tops are five repetitions of the seven Fano lines sorted({i,(i+1) mod 7,(i+3) mod 7}), with repetition outside and i=0..6 inside. Tops are distinct even if their past subsets coincide. There are no other relations. The JSON contains explicit covers for both orders and B's exact rational coordinates(t,x,y,z), in point order.

Why A cannot embed: let Q(t,x,y,z)=x^2+y^2+z^2-t^2 and eta=diag(-1,1,1,1). Lift each of the seven bottom points x_i to(-2 eta x_i,Q(x_i)) in R^5. Radon's theorem gives disjoint nonempty convex combinations of these seven lifts with equal value. At least one support S has at most three points. A has a top T above exactly S. The identity Q(T-x_i)=Q(T)+(-2 eta x_i).T+Q(x_i) is affine in the lift. Its weighted average is strictly negative on S and nonnegative on the other support because incomparable bottom/top pairs cannot be timelike in either direction. Equality of the convex combinations is impossible. This proof is restricted to 3+1 and does not exclude embeddings in all higher dimensions.
