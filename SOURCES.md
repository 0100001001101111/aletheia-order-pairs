# Sources and scope

- Yasaman K. Yazdi and Achim Kempf, *Towards Spectral Geometry for Causal Sets*, Class. Quantum Grav. 34 (2017) 094001. https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.09947
- Yasaman K. Yazdi, Marco Letizia and Achim Kempf, *Lorentzian Spectral Geometry with Causal Sets*, Class. Quantum Grav. 38 (2021) 015011; preprint 2020. Sections 2–3 define the fixed spectral quantities. Section 4's perturbations are outside this test. https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02291
- Edwin R. van Dam and Willem H. Haemers, *Which graphs are determined by their spectrum?*, Linear Algebra and its Applications 373 (2003), 241–272. Figure 2 supplies the underlying 30-element example's graphs. https://repository.tilburguniversity.edu/bitstreams/a0757d00-8071-4ed5-90dc-8be8ac9dd47b/download
- Edward R. Scheinerman, *A Note on Planar Graphs and Circle Orders*, SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 4 (1991), 448–451. https://doi.org/10.1137/0404040
- J.B. Sidney, S.J. Sidney and J. Urrutia, *Circle orders, n-gon orders and the crossing number*, Order 5 (1988), 1–10. This is prior circle-order work, not a claimed new result here.
- P.C. Fishburn and W.T. Trotter, *Geometric containment orders: a survey*, Order 15 (1999), 167–182. Theorem 15 states the planar graph/vertex-edge circle-order equivalence. https://trotter.math.gatech.edu/papers/106.pdf

Access limits: the circle-order argument was checked against Scheinerman's publisher abstract and the full Fishburn–Trotter survey. The full Scheinerman and Sidney–Sidney–Urrutia original articles were not read for this reproduction. No claim to a complete prior-art search is made.
